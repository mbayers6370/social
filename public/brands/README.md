# Music service marks

Original, unmodified black icons downloaded from the providers' official asset packs for links to their song pages. Marks belong to their respective owners.

- `apple-music.svg`: Apple Music 2020 icon pack, `Apple_Music_Icon_blk_sm_073120.svg`. [Apple Music identity guidelines and icon downloads](https://marketing.services.apple/apple-music-identity-guidelines).
- `spotify.svg`: Spotify's 2024 icon pack, `Primary_Logo_Black_RGB.svg`. [Spotify design guidelines and icon downloads](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design).

Use the supplied artwork with its original aspect ratio and sufficient clear space. Service icons render at full opacity and 24px tall on a light background, separated from text. These static files make no external requests and contain no scripts, external references, player or tracking code.

- `itunes-store.svg`: original `iTunes_Store_Small_Badge_RGB_012318.svg` from Apple’s [US/UK small-badge pack](https://www.apple.com/105/media/us/ipoditunes/itunes/2018/3a5387c5_1ebd_4993_9633_ff7264dc27bf/itunes-store-badges/us_uk.zip). See [iTunes identity guidelines](https://marketing.services.apple/itunes-identity-guidelines). Preserve its source bytes and aspect ratio. The shared badge renders at its native 79.25×16px size with 2px horizontal and 14px vertical clear space, exceeding one-tenth of its height, within a 44px link target. Apple expressly permits the small badge for limited space and titles in lists, but does not state a separate numeric minimum for it; using its native size is our compact-list interpretation, not an Apple-confirmed minimum. It sits beneath the artist name in On Repeat and the song dialog; search results give it a separate row below the selection button to keep both tap targets independent, linking to the API-provided song URL with `app=itunes` to select the store destination.

Apple’s [Search API terms](https://performance-partners.apple.com/search-api) restrict supplied artwork to promoting the corresponding content with a nearby linked purchase badge. iTunes-sourced songs therefore show the purchase badge without additional streaming-service links. No audio or video previews are used. Apple trademark credits appear once on `/copyright#music-credits`, accessible through the shared Copyright footer link. These implementation measures do not represent Apple approval or a legal determination about the product’s overall use.
