Noto Sans Regular is bundled for portable account PDFs with embedded fonts.

Source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-fonts/blob/main/hinted/ttf/NotoSans/NotoSans-Regular.ttf
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).
The demo uses bundled Inter for body/UI text and Margin Aside for headings, branding and posts. It makes no runtime font-service requests. Noto Sans is used for portable PDFs; its license does not apply to the original Margin font families.

Custom Margin Hinge and Margin Loose Mono font families, their specimen and their separate usage notes are under `margin/`. Hinge and Loose Mono are retained design studies; the active interface loads the WOFF2 faces under `margin-aside/` through `next/font/local`. Optional specimen stylesheets remain separate. Editable build sources are in `design/fonts/margin/`.
