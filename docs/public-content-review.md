## Publication decisions still required

- Confirm legal operator, service address, official domain, working support/privacy/safety contacts and responsible owners. `support@example.com` remains a visibly labelled, unmonitored placeholder.
- Decide launch jurisdictions and have qualified counsel establish applicable requirements. An 18+ product concept does not decide every child-safety obligation.
- Complete the deployed provider/data/cookie inventory, purposes, legal grounds, storage/transfer arrangements and contracts.
- Set and verify content, message, Notebook, security-record, report, account and backup retention; define restore/redeletion handling and narrow exceptions.
- Establish rights-request identity checks, delivery, response timing, complaint routes and full provider/account erasure.
- Review the content licence, consumer protections, service changes, closure, liability and disputes. No final licence, arbitration or waiver was invented in these drafts.
- Establish reporting, reviewer permissions, evidence retention, decision notices, appeals and urgent escalation.
- Settle applicable copyright procedures, designated-agent requirements, notices, counter-notices and repeat-infringer handling before uploads open.
- Assign an accessibility review scope, target and feedback process.
- Complete authorised legal and operational review before adding effective dates or accepting account terms.

## Primary review references

Checked 30 October 2026. These references informed issue identification and drafting; they do not establish which laws apply to Margin. No source text was copied into a policy.

- [ICO: Right to be informed](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/individual-rights/right-to-be-informed/) identifies privacy-notice topics including purposes, retention and recipients. The page itself notes that guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act; region-specific legal review remains necessary.
- [FTC: Privacy and Security](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security) supports checking privacy promises against actual practices and limiting collection and retention.
- [U.S. Copyright Office: Section 512 resources](https://www.copyright.gov/512/) explains notice/counter-notice concepts and conditional safe-harbour requirements. The draft does not assert eligibility or publish an unverified agent.
- [W3C: WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) supplies an accessibility assessment reference. Source-level provisions and limited public-page checks are not a conformance determination.
