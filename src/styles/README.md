# Stylesheet conventions

`globals.css` owns Tailwind layers, base typography, native fields, focus, and global motion preferences. `application.css` is the single ordered feature-style manifest; it loads shared components before their consumers. `tokens.css` defines the palette, type scale, spacing, and shared layout geometry.

Each full selector has one owning stylesheet. Keep its responsive, interaction, and reduced-motion variants together. Feature-specific contextual selectors belong with that feature. Run `npm run styles:check` to catch repeated imports, orphan stylesheets, and conflicting ownership.

## Shared design rules

- Body copy, forms, labels, and controls use Inter. Headings and the wordmark use Caudex. Section headings use title case, such as Recent Posts and Your Photographs; body copy remains in sentence case.
- Entry text and labels start at 1rem. Small notes share 0.75rem, 400-weight Inter, 1.5 line height, and `--note-foreground`.
- Use spacing tokens: related details 8px, groups 24px, sections 32px, and larger breaks 48px.
- Divider lines inside cards and menus run edge to edge; inset the content instead of the line.
- Action buttons use the shared `--radius-control` (8px), including icon buttons. Avatars and status dots remain circular; message bubbles and photographs retain their own shapes. Messages use the envelope icon; post conversations use the speech bubble.
- Interactive controls retain at least 44px targets, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion alternatives.
- Dropdowns and selection lists inset their rounded hover/focus surfaces by `--menu-inset`; hover fills never meet the menu shell edge.
- Photographs use the shared 12px radius. Preserve full photographs in uncropped viewers and maintain aspect ratios.
- Card divider lines span the full inner width, edge to edge. Pad the content inside each section rather than insetting its divider.

## Palette and branding

Parchment `#FAF9F6`, Ink `#30383D`, Citron `#B9C65A`, and Blackened Olive `#454D24` are the base palette. Navigation uses Olive with Parchment labels; selected mobile destinations use Citron icons and labels without a background highlight. Notification dots use Citron.

Shared home links use the lowercase Caudex wordmark and angled framing marks. Mobile uses a smaller wordmark. Browser and Apple icons use only the two framing marks. Original brand artwork remains unmodified.

## Surface hierarchy

Use Citron for invitations and primary actions. The collapsed feed composer uses one neutral outer border, a 36px avatar, an Inter body prompt with 12px of text inset and no inner border, and a neutral outlined photograph button with an olive icon. It expands in document flow from 1024px; narrower viewports use a full-screen composer. Both presentations share the same form, tab-level drafts, allowances and Publish action. Cancel preserves drafts and returns focus. Expanded composer header/footer dividers run edge to edge across the card, with padded content inside. Attachment controls and remaining allowances share a compact row; posting guidance stays in its disclosure. The profile’s New Post control uses Citron with a Blackened Olive border and text. Desktop file dropping starts the photograph flow after validation and allowance checks; an image description remains required. Profile-section headings share the plain card surface with their content, keeping their divider lines, spacing and Blackened Olive motifs. Feed author details, writing and photographs also use the plain warm card surface without a colored author band. The warm olive paper token remains available for quiet friendship status. Do not add another tinted band to every post inside a profile's existing reading stream.

On Repeat, Gallery and Recent Posts keep their cards, plain title rows and divider lines in both empty and populated states. Menus use the shared inset and rounded control shape, including their focus/hover states. Device photograph selection keeps one real keyboard-operable native input over a neutral outlined surface with olive icons, with a visible filename and choose/change action. Do not advertise drag-and-drop without implementing it.

## Component ownership

`SectionTitle` and `SectionMotif` align section labels and decorative icons. Shared avatars own their initial crop and circular frame. `PostCard`, `PostFooter`, and `PostCardSkeleton` share post geometry; Conversation sits at the top right beside the plain author details on main-feed posts; below 640px it keeps its accessible name and uses an icon-only 44px target. Profile posts keep Conversation in the footer beside owner Edit. The conversation animation preview uses the same placement as its source post. Post cards omit the Friends audience label. Profile posts omit the repeated author portrait/name and place the darker posted date before the muted expiry date, separated by a decorative dot. New Post leads the owner identity actions before Edit and Settings at all screen sizes.

Profile uses a sticky identity/details rail and a single recent-post stream from 72rem when space permits. Narrow windows and larger reading preferences stack identity, On Repeat, Gallery, then Recent Posts in that document order. Gallery uses a compact row of up to three square thumbnails before the post stream; narrow layouts use 64px song artwork. Edit profile is available in the header menu and beside Settings as a quiet outlined icon button on your own profile. Both entry points turn the identity into an inline form: Name beside the portrait, Bio beneath it, then Save changes and Cancel. Saving applies validated tab-local edits; Cancel or Escape discards the draft and returns focus to the entry point. The rest of the page remains available, without a modal or focus trap. Settings handles username changes separately: 3–24 ASCII letters, numbers or underscores, starting with a letter, normalized to lowercase. Availability excludes other sample profiles and reserved service names; confirmation commits the new handle with a seven-day cooldown. Profile links and friendships retain their stable person IDs. These changes and cooldowns live only in tab memory, reset on refresh, and do not claim production account-wide uniqueness. Friendship controls are reserved for other profiles. Mobile profile content begins 24px below the navigation. Portrait, name and handle share the first row; the bio sits beneath, followed by compact friendship, message and safety actions. Friends is a noninteractive two-person status with a visible Friends label, soft olive background and dark olive text. Add friend uses a person-plus icon; Message stays a quiet outlined envelope with an accessible name. Unfriend lives in the overflow menu. Relationship feedback occupies its own line below the complete action row. Successful friendship confirmations clear after six seconds on profiles and the Friends page; a newer confirmation restarts that delay. Relationship labels and errors remain until their state changes. Nonfriend and pending-request profiles retain all three section cards with friends-only explanations; they render no posts, photographs, song rows or content links. Incoming-request Decline uses an outlined control on profiles. Inaccessible galleries omit View More. Profile overflow uses a mobile action sheet and a desktop anchored menu; destructive actions require confirmation.

Mobile navigation contains Feed, Messages, and Profile with matching icon spaces. Its row is 52px high, plus shell padding and the device safe area. Search, Notifications, and the hamburger account menu sit in the header; desktop uses the profile avatar. The menu and notifications share mutually exclusive state. The hamburger morphs to a close icon with a reduced-motion alternative. Its navigation panel is portalled outside profile/card stacking contexts and positioned against the header trigger.

Post previews use full-width, centered 3:2 crops for single photographs, including portraits. Multiple-photo posts use matching 3:2 tiles: two across on desktop, with a wider swipeable tile and a glimpse of the next on mobile. Source proportions are retained in the uncropped full-image viewer. Responsive image sizes match these rendered widths.

Gallery keeps the existing natural-proportion featured layout on mobile and uses a denser three-column More Photographs grid. Desktop browsing uses uniform, centered 3:2 thumbnail crops: three larger featured images above five smaller columns. Full-photo viewers always show the uncropped image. Load the first three browsing photographs eagerly and defer the rest.

Arrange and Add Photograph open a compact editor with three featured slots and rounded-square thumbnails for the other photographs. Mobile uses the same scrim, rounded top, bottom anchoring, safe-area padding, Cancel, and focus-trapped modal behavior as profile sheets; desktop uses a centered modal with six additional-photo columns. Content scrolls internally while the selection controls and Done/Cancel stay visible. Dragging scrolls that internal viewport, never the page behind it; keyboard and tap controls remain alternatives.

Edits are drafts until Done: reorder, feature, add, replacement, and confirmed deletion commit together after ownership, capacity, current-gallery and daily allowance checks. Cancel, Escape, the close button, and outside dismissal discard gallery edits. The photo picker shares the composer’s plain surface, rounded controls and edge-to-edge header/footer dividers. Its library and upload fields scroll internally while Cancel and Use Photograph stay visible in a single footer linked to the upload form. Mobile uses a bottom sheet for both gallery and post selection; desktop uses a centered picker. Library previews use consistent landscape crops. Selection stays in the picker with an outline, checkmark and pressed state until Use Photograph confirms it; that action stays disabled without an eligible selection or device file. Reusing a library photograph preserves its saved description and shows it read-only. Changing to a new device file clears the old description. Device-file previews show the full selected image before attachment. Its description field is explicitly labeled as alt text and explains its screen-reader purpose. Gallery additions use the shared photo allowance without consuming a post slot or automatically publishing to Feed. Gray out Feature photograph when three featured slots are filled; leave Unfeature available. Saving closes the editor without a redundant success message. Show a single Delete photograph action; omit Remove from gallery. Full-gallery replacement retains the old photo in its library and posts and explains that distinction. Deletion removes owned image attachments, gallery entries, photo library entries and draft references while retaining post text and conversations. Changes remain tab-local; no server or fixture asset is deleted, and sample data returns on refresh.

Messages uses a white page canvas and white inbox previews with thin neutral-gray borders. Hover uses a cool gray; selection is highlighted only on desktop. On desktop, header, message panels and footer fit within the viewport; inbox and history scroll internally. Keep these page styles separate from the conversation's warm background, message bubbles, fonts, and composer layout. Show one responsive Local preview notice, with details on demand; keep explanatory paragraphs out of the message history. Unsent message drafts live in the tab-level provider across navigation and clear on refresh. The viewer’s one-way Notebook stays pinned above other inbox conversations. Its book icon uses the same circular avatar shape and responsive sizes as other threads. Entries use the regular outgoing message bubbles, spacing and timestamp grouping. New notebook entries also stay in tab memory across navigation and clear on refresh; they are never sent to a recipient.

The selected desktop feed post and conversation share geometry tokens. The post is muted without dimming photographs. Profile conversations use an inline flip; mobile conversations use a full-screen flip. Forms and metadata stay outside the independently scrolling discussion.

The public footer contains the grouped Explore, Support and Legal links plus the copyright row in Blackened Olive. It omits the redundant logo and repeated primary-navigation row. Application utility links remain in the account menu and Settings, with a compact desktop footer.

Public information pages share an editorial opening, a short context summary and a readable main column. From 1024px, the section index stays beside the content below the sticky header; narrow screens stack it in reading order. Policy pages retain a visible draft notice and expandable unresolved-review details. Related links and the footer connect the practical and policy pages. Content remains server-rendered, with no additional client state for the document layout.

The welcome page is an editorial sequence led by one framed feed, offset against a large Caudex headline from 1024px and stacked below it on smaller screens. A Blackened Olive photographic interlude separates the finite feed from the later message exchange; the gallery shows static public sample photographs in three equal columns on mobile and staggered photographs on desktop. Images have no click actions or expand icons. Keep passages short, vary the proportions between sections, and preserve the product’s paper, olive and citron palette. The See how it feels anchor reaches the feed below the sticky desktop header. The joining section links into invitation-code signup. Join Margin leads to /sign-up and Sign in leads to /sign-in; /join redirects to signup. All examples use fictional public content, never app data or messaging endpoints. The conversation card flips in place with focus moving to its back control and supports keyboard input; core content renders on the server. One entrance is confined to the lead feed and respects reduced motion. Mobile keeps the brand, Sign in and Join Margin in the header. The footer contains grouped links and copyright without duplicate navigation or a logo.

Settings uses relative type throughout, with 15px-equivalent controls and 14px-equivalent helpers at the default browser size. Reading preferences scale the root so navigation, dialogs, fields and profile text enlarge together. Quiet outlined account actions distinguish Sign in and Sign out (Exit preview locally); no confirmation is needed for this reversible session action. Post flags retain accessible names and a hover/focus tooltip that Escape dismisses. Local report actions say Save report until delivery to moderation is connected.
